# Product

## Purpose

Q Intelligence is Q Media’s central source of truth for targeted client acquisition in Ottawa. It turns fragmented public research, qualification, contacts, personalized outreach, provider activity, and responses into one evidence-backed operating record with a clear next action.

## Users

Primary users are Q Media’s founders, strategists, researchers, and sales team. Approved AI research agents can add or update structured research, but cannot send. Authorized human senders own final message approval and the relationship.

## Success definition

At any time a user can answer:

- Which businesses are the strongest current opportunities?
- What direct evidence supports that ranking?
- Is a verified or usable email available?
- Has this business actually been contacted?
- Which campaign and sending account were used?
- What exact message version was approved?
- Did the prospect reply, and was the reply positive?
- What should happen next?
- Have 10 new qualified email-ready businesses been contacted today?

The daily goal is achieved only from confirmed first sends, not research records or queued campaign leads.

## Positioning

Know the business before you speak, then keep the relationship state in the same evidence-led system.

## Product principles

1. Evidence before confidence. Consequential claims expose source, date, and confidence.
2. Quality before volume. Research continues until the email-ready minimum is real, without turning outreach into a mass campaign.
3. Research and contact are separate milestones.
4. Que Media fit outranks generic marketing need.
5. Humans own the relationship. Every email’s campaign, sender, recipient, subject, body, and evidence are reviewed before submission.
6. Scores explain; they do not decide.
7. Missing remains missing. The product never fabricates contacts, metrics, activity, replies, or urgency.
8. Provider failure never erases the local lead.
9. Operational changes and overrides are auditable.
10. Credentials never reach client-side code or storage.

## Product boundary

Q Intelligence is a targeted research, lead-management, and personalized outreach system. It is not a purchased-list tool, an automatic email generator, or an unsupervised bulk sequencer. Instantly provides delivery infrastructure for individually reviewed messages. Research agents do not receive send permission.

## Accessibility and privacy

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic controls, screen-reader-friendly evidence and charts, non-colour status cues, touch targets, zoom/reflow, and reduced motion.

Collect public business data with professional restraint. Decision-maker context must be public and outreach-relevant. Preserve provenance and uncertainty, and support correction or removal through an authorized workflow.
