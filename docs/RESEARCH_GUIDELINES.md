# Q Intelligence Ottawa lead research guidelines

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-07-28

This is the central operating standard for every Q Media human researcher and approved AI research agent. A record must meet the same standard regardless of who created it.

## Daily outcome

Each research cycle must add at least 10 newly qualified commercial leads with a verified or usable public business email. Continue researching until that threshold is reached.

Strong leads without email should also be added. They do not satisfy the email-ready minimum. For example, a valid cycle may add 14 businesses: 10 qualified email-ready businesses and four strong businesses whose email remains unavailable.

“Researched,” “qualified,” and “contacted” are separate states. Adding a record never counts as contacting it.

## Market and client fit

Focus on Ottawa and realistic surrounding markets Q Media can serve. Prefer privately operated, revenue-generating businesses with a plausible need for short-form video, content creation, social management, branding, local SEO, Google Business Profile support, advertising, launch campaigns, photography, websites, or broader digital marketing.

Small businesses are eligible when the need, decision access, commercial potential, and fit are credible. Exclude public schools, hospitals, government departments, public agencies, and other organizations clearly outside Q Media’s practical sales profile. A franchise location can qualify when local decision access or a locally controlled marketing opportunity is plausible.

Give priority to businesses that are:

- opening in days, weeks, or months;
- recently opened, reopening, or changing ownership;
- relocating, expanding, renovating, or adding a location;
- rebranding or launching a new service;
- visibly under-marketed relative to their offer;
- preparing for a season, event, announcement, or other time-sensitive growth window.

## Where to search

Use a diverse source mix. Start with first-party sources and corroborate time-sensitive claims where possible.

1. Official business websites, contact pages, location pages, blogs, newsrooms, menus, booking pages, and domain-linked forms.
2. Official Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, and other public business profiles.
3. Google Business Profile and legitimate business directories.
4. Ottawa-focused news, food, retail, culture, and business publications.
5. Neighbourhood BIAs, municipal updates, community associations, local event pages, and commercial-development announcements.
6. Shopping-centre, leasing, construction, franchise, and property-development announcements.
7. Public Facebook pages and groups, Reddit discussions, restaurant discovery pages, halal food pages, local blogs, and community newsletters.
8. Press releases and credible ownership or launch announcements.

Community discussion is a discovery signal, not proof of an official fact. Use the business’s own public surface or a credible publication to corroborate the identity, status, address, opening timing, and contact information.

## Minimum record

Before submission, collect:

- official business name and commercial category;
- Ottawa-area location, neighbourhood, and address when available;
- official website or strongest official public surface;
- opening or growth status, date, and confidence;
- plain-language business description, services, and products;
- every relevant public social profile;
- public owner or decision-maker details when professionally relevant;
- phone, email, alternative email, and contact form when available;
- at least three specific observations;
- at least two direct evidence sources;
- discovery reason, fit reason, likely marketing needs, service angle, outreach approach, urgency, confidence, and research notes;
- scores for every published scoring factor;
- researcher/agent identity, process, batch ID, and research time.

Every contact, observation, decision maker, and outreach fact must cite a URL included in the evidence collection.

## Email search and labels

Search email in this order:

1. official website header, footer, contact, team, press, location, and privacy pages;
2. official social profile bio or contact action;
3. Google Business Profile and official directory listings;
4. press releases and credible business announcements;
5. legitimate third-party directories;
6. public domain records only when appropriate and professionally relevant.

Never invent an email. Never convert a guessed pattern into a confirmed contact.

Use these provenance labels:

- `official`: published on a first-party business surface;
- `third-party-listed`: published by a legitimate independent source;
- `inferred`: derived from a pattern and not confirmed;
- `unavailable`: no email found.

Use these verification labels:

- `verified`: corroborated and suitable for use;
- `usable`: publicly listed and plausible, but not fully corroborated;
- `unverified`: not safe for outreach without review;
- `invalid`: known bad, bounced, malformed, or withdrawn;
- `unavailable`: absent.

An inferred email must remain `unverified` and cannot make a lead email-ready. Record role-based, general-business, or personal-business contact type. If email is missing, preserve the best phone or contact form and set the next action to find or request the correct business email.

## Evidence standard

Evidence entries store the direct URL, page title, publisher, concise note, source kind, publication date when available, discovery date, supported claims, and confidence.

- Link to the exact article or page, not a search-results page.
- Capture what the source actually proves, not a broader interpretation.
- Store publication and discovery dates separately.
- Mark dates as unknown or estimated when they are not confirmed.
- Prefer the latest first-party source when sources conflict.
- Preserve important conflicts in research notes; do not silently discard them.
- Recheck high-impact sources that are old, redirected, or inaccessible.
- Do not claim a social or website weakness without a direct observation.
- Do not turn missing metrics into zero.

The ingestion service checks up to 20 submitted source URLs. Fewer than two reachable sources or the absence of a reachable official source prevents automatic qualification.

## Three required observations

Each observation contains:

1. the directly observed fact;
2. the business or marketing implication;
3. one or more evidence URLs.

Good: “The official opening announcement identifies a September launch, creating a short launch-campaign window.”
Weak: “They need better marketing.”
Not allowed: unsupported praise, imagined financial performance, guessed customer sentiment, or invented technical issues.

## Duplicate prevention

Search Q Intelligence before adding a candidate. Compare:

- normalized business name;
- official domain;
- social profile URLs and handles;
- phone number;
- street address;
- every email address.

If a strong identity signal matches, update the existing lead with the new source and observation. Do not create another record. A name-only match is ambiguous and requires a stronger identity field or human resolution. When a new source changes opening timing, confidence, fit, or urgency, preserve the source history and recalculate the score.

## Qualification gate

A lead can be auto-qualified only when:

- it is a realistic commercial client in the Ottawa-area service market;
- the structured input is valid;
- it has at least three cited observations and two evidence sources;
- at least two submitted evidence sources are reachable;
- at least one reachable source is first-party/official;
- the transparent quality score is at least 65;
- no strong exclusion or duplicate rule is triggered.

An email is not required for a strong lead to be stored. Only `verified` or `usable` emails count toward the research-cycle email-ready target.

## Lead-quality scoring

Score every input from 0 to 5. Use `0` for genuinely absent or strongly negative evidence, not for an unmeasured fact unless the missing evidence itself is the limitation.

| Factor | Weight | A 5 means |
|---|---:|---|
| Recency | 12 | a very recent, current opportunity |
| Opening or expansion | 12 | confirmed launch, move, expansion, or equivalent |
| Marketing need | 12 | strong, directly observed opportunity |
| Q Media fit | 14 | multiple realistic Q Media service angles |
| Decision-maker access | 8 | a relevant person and usable route are public |
| Email reliability | 12 | official, verified public business email |
| Social opportunity | 6 | observable, actionable content gap |
| Website opportunity | 6 | observable, actionable website/local gap |
| Likely budget | 7 | credible commercial capacity, supported by context |
| Business scale | 5 | enough operating scale or growth potential |
| Urgency | 3 | action window is short and credible |
| Evidence strength | 3 | strong first-party and corroborating evidence |

The weighted total is out of 100. The interface shows every factor, missing flag, rationale, evidence link, model version, and grade. The score prioritizes review; it does not replace judgment.

## Personalized draft standard

Draft only from facts already stored in the record. A useful email:

- identifies one meaningful, verifiable observation or timing signal;
- connects that observation to a realistic Q Media service;
- explains relevance without generic praise;
- asks for a low-friction next step;
- avoids promises, fabricated results, false urgency, and repetitive name-swapping;
- records the evidence IDs used for personalization.

The final sender, recipient, campaign, subject, body, and supporting research are always reviewed by an authorized user before Instantly submission.

## Structured submission

Use `POST /api/research/leads` with:

- `Authorization: Bearer $Q_INTELLIGENCE_INTERNAL_API_TOKEN`;
- `Content-Type: application/json`;
- actor headers when required by the calling process;
- a body conforming to [`schemas/research-lead.schema.json`](schemas/research-lead.schema.json).

Use an active `batchId` in `trace.batchId`. The service returns a new record, an update to an exact duplicate, a validation failure with field paths, or a conflict when identity is ambiguous. AI agents do not bypass source checks, scoring, duplicate resolution, or qualification.

## End-of-cycle checklist

- At least 10 newly added records are both qualified and email-ready.
- Every additional strong lead without email was retained with an alternate route and next action.
- No duplicate business was created.
- Every claim and contact points to stored evidence.
- Uncertain facts are labelled uncertain.
- Personalization drafts use stored facts only.
- The cycle completion endpoint accepts the batch.

If any check fails, keep the cycle active and continue researching.
